# StarTrack project records

StarTrack records projects and their evolving submissions, with accountable
attribution to the people who use the application.

## Language

**Account**: A person's application identity. Deactivation removes access without
erasing their historical attribution.

**Project**: A continuing body of work whose identity remains the same when its
name or details change. Different projects may have the same name.
_Avoid_: Treating a project name as its identity

**Project version**: A retained snapshot of a project's submitted details. Saving
an update creates a new version rather than changing an earlier snapshot.
_Avoid_: Using project and version interchangeably

**Archive**: Removal of a project from active work lists while preserving its
versions and attribution. Archiving is not permanent erasure.

**Reviewed history mapping**: An explicitly reviewed assignment of historical
submissions to projects and their version order. Matching names alone do not
establish that submissions belong to the same project.
